## 📖 The Mission

At U.S. Embassies and Consulates across 140+ countries, Marine Security Guards stand post 24/7/365. Every detachment mints their own custom challenge coins, PT shirts, and morale patches. 

Historically, trading or buying these coins required physically knowing someone standing post or exchanging them at the diplomatic bar. **Detcoins** opens this world to everyone:

- **100% Direct MWR Funding**: Proceeds go straight into each detachment's Morale, Welfare, & Recreation (MWR) pot to fund high-altitude rucks, summit climbs, charity drives, and Marine Corps Ball tickets.
- **Unclassified Authenticity**: Only genuine detachment-minted gear and verified unclassified field stories from the Marines themselves.

## ⚡ The Ecosystem (Three Fronts)


### 1. 🎖️ For Marines (Detachment Portal)
- **Case Management**: Add, price, and adjust live inventory for challenge coins, watch PT tees, PVC morale patches, and station magnets.
- **MWR Goal & Earnings Radar**: Real-time tracking of funds raised, units sold, and direct supporter orders.
- **Adventure Journal**: Post photos and stories of rucks and charity drives funded by coin proceeds (e.g. *Mount Takao Pre-Dawn Ruck*, *Orphanage Holiday Supply Drop*).
- **Det of the Week Submissions**: Dispatch unclassified mission stories and frames to compete for the homepage hero spotlight.

### 2. 🇺🇸 For Civilians & Collectors (The Exchange)
- **Global Post Directory**: Filter and search by embassy post, city, or MSG Region (*Region 2 South America, Region 3 East Asia, Region 4 Europe, Region 8 East Africa*, etc.).
- **Mission Attribution**: See exactly what adventure your purchase is funding before checking out.
- **Collector Tiers**: Track limited batches, numbered die runs, and detachment exclusives.

### 3. 🤝 For Verified Vendors (The Supply Network)
- **Live Inventory Radar**: Track rough coin quantities across every embassy detachment in real time to spot supply shortages before they run out.
- **Resupply & Sponsorship Proposals**: Pitch custom die strikes, 150–300 unit restock runs, and artwork sponsorships directly to detachment commanders.
- **Diplomatic Logistics**: Direct coordination for shipments to diplomatic pouch and APO/FPO addresses.

## 🌟 Flagship Features

### 🏆 Det of the Week
Every week, detachments submit unclassified stories and frames of squad adventures. The winning detachment takes the homepage hero slot, with their official case sponsored **front and center** for maximum community backing.

### 📰 The Weekly Watchstander
The digital blog-o-sphere for motivators. Curates field reports, trail rucks, embassy handover traditions, and links detachment Instagram channels to drive organic credibility and community engagement.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: [Next.js 16](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router, Turbopack, React 19)
- **Request Interception**: `proxy.ts` (Next.js 16 proxy convention)
- **Database & Auth**: [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) (PostgreSQL, Row-Level Security, `@supabase/ssr`)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS v4](https://tailwindcss.com/) & Radix/Base UI primitives
- **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Typography**: Newsreader (Editorial Serif) + Geist Sans & Mono
